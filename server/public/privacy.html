Privacy policy

Who is responsible

The data controller is Eryk Wlodarski. Contact: e.wlodarski@protonmail.com.

The AdMeNot program

The current version of AdMeNot sends no data anywhere. Scans, reports and the action journal stay on your computer.

The only exception is the diagnostic command admenot selfcheck --online, which you run yourself. It checks whether our server is reachable by calling /api/v1/health. The request carries nothing beyond a standard HTTP request with the header User-Agent: AdMeNot/<version>.

This website

The website uses no cookies, no analytics and no resources from other servers.

It is hosted by Cloudflare, which acts as our processor. Cloudflare sees your IP address when you connect, because that is how the internet works. We do not store or log IP addresses, and our code does not read them.

The installer is downloaded from GitHub, which has its own privacy statement.

Legal basis and transfers

We process this data to deliver the website and the server and to keep them secure. The legal basis is our legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.

Cloudflare, Inc. is based in the United States, so data may be processed outside the EEA. The transfer is based on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework, in which Cloudflare participates.

We keep no data from visits. Cloudflare keeps technical data for the period set in its terms of service as our processor.

Rules for every future feature

  • We do not store IP addresses.
  • The IMEI, serial number, phone numbers and app list of a customer's phone are sent only with explicit consent at the moment of sending (for example, an error report you see before it is sent).
  • Usage statistics (telemetry) only after you opt in.
  • Every new feature that sends data is added to this policy first.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you may ask for access to your data, its correction or deletion, restriction of its processing, its portability, and object to its processing. Write to the address above. You may also lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority — in Poland, the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO).
